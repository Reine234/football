HOW TO PLAY FansBetLiga?
How to play
1- Choose a league. Currently (Premier League, Serie A, La Liga, Bundesliga, Ligue 1). Coming soon:
  1. International (World Cup, European Championship, Africa Cup of Nations, Copa America, Asian Cup, Champions League)
  2. Europe (Premier League, Serie A, La Liga, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Netherlands, Portugal, Turkey, Belgium, Russia, etc.)
  3. Africa (Egypt, Morocco, Algeria, South Africa, Tunisia, Nigeria, DR Congo, Angola, Tanzania, Sudan, Côte d’Ivoire, Cameroon, Senegal, Ghana…)
  4. Americas (Brazil, Argentina, Mexico, Colombia, Uruguay, Ecuador, Chile, Paraguay, Peru, Bolivia, Venezuela, Costa Rica, USA, Canada)
  5. Asia (Saudi Arabia, Japan, South Korea, China, Qatar, Australia, Iran, UAE, …)
Note: Languages: English, Spanish, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Turkish, French, German, Portuguese, Dutch, etc.
2- Go to Home, select the Matchday, and enter a score for each match (e.g., 2–1).
3- You can edit your scores until kickoff for each match. Once the match starts, it becomes locked. After entering all scores, tap “Done” — your matchday predictions appear, you review them, then confirm. If you’re already registered, your selected matches (day/time) and predictions will be shown. If you’re not registered yet, the system will ask you to sign up (three options: Email, Google, or Facebook).
4- When official results are published, your points are calculated and visible in Results (per match and total). Every football fan can play up to this point without paying for a license.
5- To view rankings in Winners — Daily (per matchday), First Round (first half of the season), and Overall (entire season) — you must pay for a license (20€ in Europe and 20$ in other continents). If a fan clicks Winners without having paid, they are redirected to the payment link. Payment can be made via PayPal, credit card, mobile money transfer, etc. Once validated, the fan can see their ranking (even with up to 1,000,000 participants, the ranking shows 49 positions: 24 above and 24 below the fan).
6- After confirming matchday predictions, the system can ask the fan if they want to stake (try to win money). Two options:
  1. Stake for one matchday only: 5€ / 5$
  2. Stake for the whole league season (matchdays + first round + end-of-season rankings): 10€/$ multiplied by the number of matches in the league.
Note: Your predictions and points are tracked by league, matchday, and season. You can play multiple leagues, but each league progresses independently.
How much can the top players win?
For simplicity, the matchday stake is 5€ per licensed fan. For a league with:
  • 1,000 licensed fans → 1,000 × 5 = 5,000€ to share
  • 10,000 licensed fans → 50,000€
  • 100,000 licensed fans → 500,000€
  • 1,000,000 licensed fans → 5,000,000€
We still need to define whether this pool is shared among the top 3 only, top 10, etc.
  • ≤ 1,000 licensed: reward between 3 and 10 people
  • > 1,000 and ≤ 10,000: reward between 50 and 100
  • > 10,000 and ≤ 100,000: reward between 100 and 500
  • > 100,000 and ≤ 1,000,000: reward between 500 and 1,000
If the top 3 include fans who did not stake, they receive winner certificates and sponsor gifts (shirts, watches, trips, etc.) or FansBetLiga gifts (stadium tickets, etc.). Fans who staked receive gifts plus financial rewards. The exact distribution is shown on the matchday page (“prizes” banner/box) when rewards are active.
